To report a problem, include your app version, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help, but please hide names, sleep records and private writing. Do not send passwords, payment card details or your full Apple Health export. We do not promise a fixed response time.

Do I need an Apple Watch or Apple Health?

No. You can enter sleep times manually. With permission, Moona can read sleep from Apple Health and write your mood rating as State of Mind. Change access in Apple Health or system settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously saved entries in Moona or Health.

Why did a reminder not arrive?

Check Moona's reminder preferences and iOS notification permissions. Focus modes, notification summaries and device settings may affect delivery. Moona schedules reminders locally; it does not send them from a Moona push server.

Where are my entries stored?

The current app configuration stores course records on your device without a Moona account. Active Moona iCloud sync is not available in this configuration. Be careful when deleting the app or changing devices: do not assume the course can be recovered from us. Apple backups and Health records are managed separately.

How do I manage my information?

Use the available profile and reminder controls, manage Health and Photos permissions in iOS, and remove any widgets you no longer want displayed. Offloading an app retains its data. Deleting Moona does not delete your Health records, images saved to Photos, shared copies or Apple backups. Email us for help finding the appropriate device controls or to make a privacy request about information we hold, such as support correspondence.

How do I save or share a recap?

Choose a recap design and use the save or share action. Saving requests add-only Photos permission. Check the card before sharing: it may contain personal sleep, energy or course progress information. To remove an exported image, delete it from Photos or the destination service as appropriate.

How do I manage a subscription?

Paid purchasing is still in development. Once subscriptions are offered, manage or cancel an Apple subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Restore eligible purchases using the same Apple Account. For App Store refund requests, use Apple's refund service. The final offer and Apple's confirmation determine price, trial eligibility and renewal terms.

Is Moona medical care?

No. Moona provides educational sleep and wellbeing tools. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about persistent sleep problems or exercises that may be unsuitable for you. The support inbox is not an emergency or medical advice service.
