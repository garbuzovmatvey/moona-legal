Moona is provided by Garbuzov Matvei, based in Kazakhstan ("we", "us"). Contact us about privacy at matveyvoronezh@gmail.com.

Moona helps you build sleep habits through lessons, check-ins, evening routines and progress summaries. The current app works without a Moona account. Your course records are processed on your device; we do not operate a server that receives those records.

Information used by the app

Depending on the features you use, Moona stores:

  • An optional name, preferred form of address and moon avatar choices.
  • Your onboarding answers about sleep difficulties, habits, goals, sleep times and Apple Watch availability.
  • Your planned schedule, reminder preferences, course start date, completed lessons, task answers and progress.
  • Sleep start/end times, the source of those times, and energy and mood ratings you enter.
  • Evening checklist entries, worries, brain-dump text, good moments and other written exercise answers.
  • Streaks, earned stickers and related dates.

This information provides your plan, lessons, reminders, charts, achievements and recap cards. Free-text entries can be sensitive; enter only what you want to keep in the app. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. The current app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Apple Health

Health access is optional and controlled by Apple's permission system. With your permission, Moona reads sleep-analysis data to estimate your main sleep session and fill in a check-in. It can write the mood rating you choose to Apple Health as State of Mind. It does not write sleep records or read your entire Health database.

You can use manual sleep entry without connecting Health. You can change Moona's Health permissions in Apple Health or system settings. Revoking permission stops the corresponding future access; it does not automatically remove previously saved Moona check-ins or records already written to Health. Manage those records separately.

Health information is used for the app's sleep and wellbeing features. We do not send HealthKit data to advertisers, data brokers or our own server, and do not use it for advertising or marketing profiles.

Storage, widgets and reminders

The current app stores its course database on your device. It does not provide an active Moona iCloud synchronization service in the current configuration. Device backups, Health synchronization and other Apple services you enable are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

To display widgets, Moona saves a small snapshot in an App Group shared with its own widget extension. It includes planned bed/wake times, course dates, task text, streak information and check-in completion dates/flags. It does not include your private journal text. Widgets and local notifications may display information on your Home Screen or Lock Screen; choose placements and notification previews accordingly. Reminders are scheduled locally and do not require our push server.

Saving and sharing results

When you save a recap image, Moona requests permission to add that image to Photos; this feature does not read your photo library. You can also choose to send recap cards through the system share sheet. Cards may contain sleep, energy or progress information. Review the image and recipient before sharing. Copies saved to Photos or sent to another app are controlled separately from Moona and are subject to the recipient's practices.

Purchases and third parties

Apple handles App Store downloads and any Apple-managed transactions under its own terms and privacy policy. Moona's current purchase scaffolding can query verified entitlements and request a restore through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card number.

RevenueCat is not integrated into the current app. Before distributing a version that uses a subscription service or another SDK, we will update this policy to identify the provider, information it receives and its purposes. The current policy must not be used to suggest that a future SDK receives no information.

If you follow a source link, open a website or share to a third-party service, that service's own privacy practices apply. Apple's privacy policy is available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments and any information you choose to include. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot and meet applicable obligations. Our support mailbox is hosted by Gmail, so email is processed through the email providers involved. Please avoid sending Health exports, private journal entries or other sensitive material unless necessary for your request. Redact screenshots where possible.

Document website

Our public document pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its processing practices. We do not add advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts or cookies to these static pages. Visiting a document page does not give us access to your Moona course records.

Retention, choices and requests

Your local course records remain available while they are retained in the app's storage. You can update settings and profile details through the available controls, revoke permissions, disable reminders and remove widgets. Deleting the app is different from offloading it: offloading preserves app data. Removing the app does not delete Health records, saved images, shared copies or Apple backups. Shared extension storage and backups may have separate lifecycles managed by iOS.

We do not hold a server-side copy of your course or journal records and cannot retrieve or remotely delete them for you. Contact matveyvoronezh@gmail.com for help locating the applicable device controls or making a privacy request. Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable legal, security or dispute obligations; unnecessary attachments should be removed when no longer needed.

Where applicable law grants them, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent privacy authority. We will handle requests under the law applicable to the request and may need to verify your identity. We do not require you to send private course records just to make a request.

For information we receive directly, our processing grounds, where such grounds are required, include handling your requested support service, legitimate interests in resolving issues and protecting the service, and compliance with legal obligations. Optional Health access and permissions remain under your control; where consent is legally required, it must be obtained before the relevant processing.

International processing and security

The operator is based in Kazakhstan. If you contact us from elsewhere, your communication may be processed there and in locations used by the email providers. Any transfers for which applicable law requires safeguards must use those safeguards. Local-only course processing does not itself send your records to the operator in Kazakhstan.

Moona uses the platform's storage and permission mechanisms. Protect your device with a passcode and keep its software updated. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and changes

Moona's course is designed for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through support. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information so we can address it.

We will revise this policy when practices change and identify the effective date of the published revision. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
